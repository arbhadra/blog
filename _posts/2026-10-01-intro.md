Welcome. This is the first entry in what I hope becomes a long-running
collection of notes, tutorials, and half-formed ideas made a little more
precise by the act of writing them down.

I built this space to think in public — to work through research problems,
record things I've learned, and occasionally share something I'm proud of.

<!--more-->

## Why a blog, and why this one

Writing forces clarity. An idea can feel complete in your head and fall
apart the moment you try to explain it to someone else. This blog is my
attempt to catch those gaps early — and to leave a trail others might
find useful.

The design here is deliberately quiet. It borrows from
[distill.pub](https://distill.pub): a serif body for comfortable reading,
a sans-serif interface, and generous space to let the content breathe.

## What you'll find here

Posts are organized by a small set of tags:

- **research** — ongoing work, questions, and findings
- **tutorial** — step-by-step walkthroughs
- **notes** — shorter, informal observations
- **writing** — longer-form essays and reflections
- **talk** — slides and summaries from presentations
- **achievement** — milestones worth marking
