I took the IELTS Academic test on 21 January 2025 and achieved an **overall
band score of 8.0**. That corresponds to CEFR level **C1** and to the IELTS
description "very good user". This post outlines what IELTS assesses, why
universities require it, and how the result compares with the official IELTS
statistics for 2024–25.

## The IELTS Academic test

IELTS (International English Language Testing System) is jointly owned by the
British Council, IDP IELTS and Cambridge University Press & Assessment. It is
recognised by universities, employers and immigration authorities worldwide.
There are two versions. **IELTS Academic** is the one used for admission to
university, and it accounts for about 79% of all IELTS test takers.

| Skill | Format | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | 4 recordings, 40 questions | ~30 min |
| Reading | 3 academic texts, 40 questions | 60 min |
| Writing | Task 1 (describe a graph or process) and Task 2 (essay) | 60 min |
| Speaking | Face-to-face interview with an examiner | 11–14 min |

Each skill is scored on a 9-band scale in half-band steps. The overall band is
the mean of the four skills, rounded to the nearest half band. Writing and
Speaking are rated against published criteria by certified examiners.
Recognising organisations verify results directly with IELTS, and the
recommended validity of a result is two years.

## Result

| Skill | Band | IELTS descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | 8.0 | Very good |
| Reading | **9.0** | Expert |
| Writing | 7.5 | Good – very good |
| Speaking | 6.5 | Competent – good |
| **Overall** | **8.0** | **Very good (CEFR C1)** |

The mean of the four skills is (8.0 + 9.0 + 7.5 + 6.5) / 4 = 7.75, which
rounds to an overall 8.0.

A band of 9.0 in Reading is the maximum possible. In the Academic Reading
section, it requires at least 39 correct answers out of 40.

IELTS does not publish official percentile ranks. As a rough guide, assume
scores are normally distributed with the published means and standard
deviations. Then Listening 8.0 lies about 1.1 SD above the mean (≈ 87th
percentile), and Reading 9.0 lies about 2.1 SD above the Academic mean
(≈ 98th percentile). These figures are estimates, not official values.

## Interpretation

- **Receptive skills are at the top of the scale.** Reading 9.0 and Listening
  8.0 show the ability to follow dense academic text and lectures. This is
  most of the day-to-day language demand of graduate study.
- **Writing 7.5 is 1.7 bands above the national mean.** It reflects
  experience in structured technical and argumentative writing.
- **Speaking 6.5 is the relative weakness** of the profile. It is still
  above the national mean, and it meets the per-skill minimum (commonly 6.0
  or 6.5) set by many programmes.
- **For admissions:** graduate programmes in English-speaking countries
  commonly require an overall band of 6.5–7.0, often with a minimum in each
  skill. An overall 8.0 is above these typical thresholds.
